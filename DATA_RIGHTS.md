# Public metadata and produced evidence

Crossref publicly permits reuse of its bibliographic metadata, while some abstracts can carry copyright. The exact request excludes abstracts, author/contact/affiliation fields and full text. Source: https://www.crossref.org/documentation/retrieve-metadata/rest-api/ . DataCite public metadata are CC0; only Findable metadata are exposed by the unauthenticated API. Sources: https://support.datacite.org/docs/harvesting-datacite-doi-metadata and https://support.datacite.org/docs/rest-api . Metadata availability is not a license to copy underlying datasets or papers.

Only minimal DOI/relationship/type facts, sanitized response/reference states and authored calculations/code/figures/manuscript may be public after checks. Exact originals, source HTML, full headers/URL queries, cookies, tracking values and screenshots remain private. No source dataset file, copyrighted abstract/full article, credential, private contact data or formal-publication manuscript enters Git. Attribute Crossref, DataCite and the source repositories without endorsement claims. The MIT code license does not relicense third-party content. Azure optimization and other formal-publication projects remain private.
